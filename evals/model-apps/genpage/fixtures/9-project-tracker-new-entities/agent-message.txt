Project tracker over the new cr_project and cr_milestone tables: portfolio summary tiles (active projects, open, due-soon and overdue milestones), searchable project cards with status badges and milestone progress, and a sortable milestone grid for the selected project with All / Open / Overdue tabs. Milestones are grouped by their cr_project lookup; rows open their Dataverse records; window cache with in-flight de-dupe and a refresh button.
