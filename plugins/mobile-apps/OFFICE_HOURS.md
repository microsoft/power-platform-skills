# Get Involved – Native Apps Office Hours

Join our native mobile apps Office Hours for developers and admins to share feedback, ask questions, and hear about upcoming features for Power Apps native mobile apps (preview). These 30-minute sessions are a great way to influence the roadmap, get help with real-world scenarios, and learn from the team and peers.

**Schedule (rest of 2026):**

* Wednesday, September 23 — 08:00–8:30 AM EST
* Wednesday, October 21 — 08:00–8:30 AM EST
* Wednesday, November 18 — 08:00–8:30 AM EST
* Wednesday, December 9 — 08:00–8:30 AM EST



We will resume the series on Wednesday, January 13, 2027. Look for future announcements here to keep up with the schedule!



**What to expect:**

* Brief updates on new features and docs
* Live Q\&A with the Native Apps team
* Share feedback and feature requests



**Come with questions about:**

* Getting started with the mobile-app plugin and the planning experience
* React Native, Expo, and TypeScript project structure
* Microsoft Entra app registration and authentication
* Connecting to Dataverse tables and Power Platform connectors
* Native device capabilities — camera, sensors, Bluetooth/NFC, background tasks
* Testing on device with the Power Apps Developer app ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/power-apps-mobile-preview/id6753083462) | [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.microsoft.PreviewApp))
* Distribution through App Store, Google Play, and MDM

**New here?** Start with the [Power Apps native mobile apps overview (preview)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-apps/mobile/native-apps/overview) to check prerequisites before your first session.
