Your AI assistant knows a lot about code. A plugin teaches it how to build on Power Platform.

You install a plugin once, and it adds a set of skills.

Each skill is one job, written down step by step. Creating a Power Pages site is one skill. Deploying it is another.

When you describe what you need, your assistant picks the right skill and follows its steps. It asks before it runs commands or changes files.

So, plugins are what you install, and skills are the jobs inside them. You just ask.
